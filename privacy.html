Privacy Policy — Fatos Sombrios Publisher

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Who uses the app

Fatos Sombrios Publisher is a personal desktop tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account "Fatos Sombrios" to publish their own videos. It has no other users and no website accounts.

2. Data we access

  • user.info.basic: the account's display name, username and avatar, shown on the review screen so the owner can confirm which account will publish.
  • video.publish: used only to upload and publish videos the owner has reviewed and approved.
  • Creator info (privacy options, interaction settings, max video length) is read before each post to show the correct options.

3. Storage

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics by third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file. No copy of TikTok data is kept elsewhere.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the account owner through the TikTok profile @meliodasogato.
